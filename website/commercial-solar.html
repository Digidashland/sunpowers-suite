Questions

Questions

?Does commercial solar get a scheme benefit?

The home scheme does not apply to commercial plants. The benefits are lower power cost and possible depreciation.

?Which model is best for a shop?

Most shops and offices choose net feeding. If approval is slow, zero export is a fast option. We advise after seeing your meter and bill.

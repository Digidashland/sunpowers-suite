How we work

How we install your plant

Every project follows the same clear path, so you always know what comes next.

Our process

From first visit to long-term care

  1. 1

    Free site visit

    Our engineer visits, measures the roof, checks shadow and your meter, and understands your bill.

  2. 2

    Blue Print and quote

    We plan the layout, often with our Blue Print tool, and give a clear written quote.

  3. 3

    Scheme, loan and paperwork

    We guide you on the government scheme for homes, bank loan documents and the net meter application.

  4. 4

    Installation

    Structure, panels, inverter, wiring and earthing are done by trained technicians.

  5. 5

    Testing and handover

    We test the plant and show you how to read generation on the app.

  6. 6

    Maintenance

    Cleaning, audits and support packages keep the plant healthy for years.

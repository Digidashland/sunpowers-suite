Why maintain

A solar plant is not fit-and-forget

A plant lives outdoors for 25 years or more. Dust, heat, rain, loose connections and ageing parts slowly reduce what it produces and can create safety risks. A regular check finds small problems before they become expensive ones.

Our audit covers the whole plant, and you can take it as a single visit or as a package that repeats every quarter, half-year or year.

What we check

A complete plant diagnosis

Panels

Cleaning, and a visual check for cracks, hotspots, discolouration and loose frames.

Earthing

Earthing connections and earth resistance, so the plant stays safe.

ACDB and DCDB

Breakers, fuses, surge protection, terminals and signs of heating or burning.

Wiring and connectors

DC and AC cables, MC4 connectors, lugs and any loose or damaged parts.

Structure

Nuts and bolts, rust, tilt and new shadows on the panels.

Inverter

Error log, settings, cooling, display and communication.

Performance check

Actual generation compared with what the plant should produce.

Audit report

A written report with findings and clear next steps.

Factory solar

Power your plant from your own roof or land

We build industrial plants from 100 kW to 2 MW on factory sheds or open ground, and help with approvals. For sites with no space, open access lets you source solar from a plant elsewhere.

Both CAPEX (you own the plant) and OPEX style options are possible. We share expected payback after studying your bill.

Flexible models

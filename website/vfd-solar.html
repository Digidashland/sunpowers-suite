Solar VFD

The motor runs directly from solar

A VFD (Variable Frequency Drive) feeds panel power straight to the motor, so no separate inverter is needed. It suits aata chakki, water pumps and cold storage from 3 to 50 HP.

Pump users may be able to apply under the PM-KUSUM scheme. Terms and eligibility are set by the government.

Simple and sturdy

Questions

Questions

?Can it run at night?

A direct-drive system runs while the sun shines. For night use you would add batteries, which raises the cost.

?How many panels for a 10 HP chakki?

Around 15 kW of solar is a common size for a 10 HP mill. We confirm after checking your motor and load.

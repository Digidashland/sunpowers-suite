About Blue Print

Plan your solar plant before you buy

Project Blue Print is a free tool. You mark your roof or land on a satellite map, pick panels and structure, and see how the plant will be laid out. You can download a blueprint PDF and ask our engineers for a quotation.

The result is a planning estimate. Our engineer confirms the final design at a site visit.

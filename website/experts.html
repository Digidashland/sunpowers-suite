Subsidy guide

PM Surya Ghar Muft Bijli Yojana

Ghar (residential) par On-Grid solar lagane walon ke liye sarkari subsidy — kaun le sakta hai, documents aur process.

Kaun le sakta hai?

  • Apna ghar aur us par residential bijli connection
  • Chhat par shadow-free jagah
  • On-Grid / Hybrid system, net metering ke saath
  • Commercial / industrial par ye subsidy nahi

Documents

  • Aadhaar card
  • Latest bijli bill
  • Bank passbook / cancelled cheque
  • Chhat ki photo
  • Aadhaar se linked mobile number

Process

  1. Portal par registration
  2. DISCOM feasibility approval
  3. Installation (registered vendor ke through)
  4. Net meter + inspection
  5. Subsidy seedha bank khate me
Subsidy ki rakam aur niyam sarkar badal sakti hai. UP me Kendra + Rajya milakar ₹1.08 lakh tak* ki jaankari hamare anubhav ke hisab se hai — latest rakam ke liye call karein. Sunpowers aavedan me poori madad karta hai (registered vendor partner ke saath).
Finance

ROI, EMI aur loan — short me

ROI — paisa kab wapas?
~3.5 saal
  • 3 kW: lagbhag 3–4 saal
  • 10 kW: ~₹4.72L net, lagbhag 3.5 saal
  • 100 kW: ~₹52L, lagbhag 3 saal (factory me 2.5)

EMI / Loan
₹2,099 /mahina se
  • SBI, HDFC, Bank of Baroda jaise bank — lagbhag 7–9% byaj
  • 3 se 7 saal tak EMI, 10–20% down payment
  • Documents: Aadhaar, PAN, bijli bill, bank statement
Finance process
3 step
  • Site visit + bill check + quote
  • Bank documents + subsidy form
  • Loan approval + installation shuru
  • Subsidy seedha bank khate me
Short me: 10 kW lagao = ~₹4.72L net → ₹10,800/mahina bachat → ~3.5 saal me paisa wapas → EMI ~₹9,800/mahina, jo bill ki bachat se nikal jati hai. (Sab anuman hai — asli rakam site aur bank par nirbhar.)
Sawal-jawab

3 kW se 500 kW tak — sab sawal-jawab

Plus par click karke jawab dekhein — short aur saaf.

Plant size3 kW se 500 kW tak kaun sa plant kiske liye best hai?
  • 3–5 kW: 2–3 BHK ghar, bill ₹3k–₹6k — 195–325 sq ft
  • 10–20 kW: bada ghar / villa — bill ₹10k–₹20k — 650–1300 sq ft
  • 30–100 kW: dukaan, office, school, hospital — bill ₹30k–₹1L
  • 100–500 kW: factory, cold storage, warehouse — bill ₹1L–₹5L
  • Ghar ke liye 3–10 kW, dukaan ke liye 20–50 kW, factory ke liye 100–500 kW best hai.
MeteringNet metering aur gross metering me kya fark hai?
  • Net metering (90% log yahi lagate hain): solar ki bijli ghar me use, bachi hui grid me, raat ko grid se wapas — bill zero
  • Gross metering: solar ki poori bijli grid ko bechte hain, ghar ki bijli alag se kharidte hain
  • Ghar / dukaan ke liye net metering lein — gross me bill zero nahi hota.
BillMera bill ₹15,000 hai, kitna kW lagana chahiye?
  • ₹15k bill ≈ 1,875 kWh/mahina — lagbhag 14 kW chahiye
  • 10 kW = 1,350 kWh = ₹10,800 bachat
  • 15 kW = 2,025 kWh = ₹16,200 bachat — bill zero + thoda extra
  • Jagah: 15 kW ke liye ~975 sq ft
Subsidy3 kW, 10 kW, 100 kW par subsidy kitni milegi?
  • Residential On-Grid / Hybrid (1–30 kW): PM Surya Ghar (Kendra) + UP Rajya — kul ₹1.08 lakh tak*
  • Commercial / Industrial par subsidy nahi — depreciation + net metering ka fayda
  • Subsidy seedha bank khate me aati hai — form bharne me Sunpowers madad karta hai
  • *Rakam sarkari niyam ke hisab se badal sakti hai — latest jaankari ke liye call karein
Jagah3 kW, 10 kW, 100 kW, 500 kW me kitni jagah lagegi?
  • 3 kW = 195 sq ft — ek kamre jitna
  • 10 kW = 650 sq ft — 2 BHK terrace
  • 100 kW = 6,500 sq ft
  • 500 kW = 32,500 sq ft (~0.75 acre) — factory shed
  • 65 sq ft per kW — south facing, shadow free
LightLight jaane par solar chalega? Raat ko?
  • On-Grid: light jaane par safety ke liye band — net metering wala yahi hai
  • Off-Grid / Hybrid: battery se chalega, raat ko bhi — battery ka kharcha extra
  • Raat ko solar nahi banta — din ki bachi bijli grid me jati hai, raat ko wapas milti hai
SeasonGarmi aur thand me production me kitna fark?
  • Garmi (March–June): sabse zyada — 10 kW par ~1,500–1,600 kWh/mahina
  • Thand (Nov–Feb): thoda kam — 10 kW par ~1,000–1,100 kWh/mahina
  • Barsaat (July–Sept): sabse kam — ~800–900 kWh/mahina
  • Saal ka average: 10 kW = 1,350 kWh/mahina (4.5 unit/kW/din)
MaintenanceMaintenance karna padta hai? Kitna kharcha?
  • Bahut kam — hafte me ek baar panel paani se dho lein
  • Hamare lagaye plant par 5 saal tak call / WhatsApp support
  • Yearly cleaning / AMC chahiye to call karein
  • Inverter apne aap chalta hai — app par roz ki bijli dekhein
LoanLoan milega? EMI kitni aayegi?
  • SBI, HDFC, BOB jaise bank — lagbhag 7–9% byaj, 10–20% down payment, 3–7 saal
  • 3 kW: lagbhag ₹2,099/mahina × 5 saal
  • 10 kW: lagbhag ₹9,800/mahina × 5 saal — bachat ₹10,800
  • Loan bank ki shart par milta hai — documents me Sunpowers madad karta hai

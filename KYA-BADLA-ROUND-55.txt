ROUND 55 — KYA BADLA (ek customer = ek quotation; brand badlein; lock par baaki band; customer ko history)
====================================================================================================
1) QUOTATION LIST: CUSTOMER KE NAAM SE
   * Ek customer ki ek hi pankti ("Ramesh Kumar · 3 quotation · Bhej di 2, Maan li 1"). Dabane par uski saari quotation khulti hain.
   * Jis customer ki sirf ek quotation hai, wo pehle jaisi ek pankti hi rehti hai.
   * Customer ke pej ka Quotation tab bhi uski saari quotation dikhata hai (band hui par "Band (dusri chuni gayi)" likha).
2) DOOSRA BRAND (Tata ke baad Adani)
   * Rok pehle jaisi: ek customer ki ek chalu quotation. Naya quotation banane par ab sandesh me saaf likha hai "usi me Option jodein".
   * Quotation me "Option jodein" = brand badlein. Naam likhne par pehle option ki copy banti hai.
   * Copy ka rate jab tak aap badal nahi dete: customer ko wo option NAHI dikhta, Accept nahi hota, aur WhatsApp/PDF "Send" band (sandesh ke saath).
   * Har option ke saamne "Stock hai / Stock kam" ka nishaan.
   * Final ke baad rate badalna (Revision) pehle jaisa hi hai: Manager/Admin, wajah likhni zaroori; customer link par "Rate badla (Revision 2): purana -> naya" dikhta hai.
3) JAB CUSTOMER EK QUOTATION LOCK (ACCEPT) KARE
   * Usi customer ki doosri, usi kaam ki (same type aur same kW) draft/sent Project quotation apne aap "Band" ho jaati hai (list me nahi dikhti;
     "Purani bhi dikhayein (N)" button se dikhti hain). Unka purana link "Ye quotation ab chalu nahi hai" dikhata hai.
   * Alag kaam (alag kW / alag type) ki quotation NAHI hatti.
   * Chuni hui quotation me chhota saar bachta hai: kya bheja gaya, kaunsa vikalp nahi chuna, customer ne kya badlav maanga, kab final hua (sirf naam, kW, rakam, tareekh).
   * Manager/Admin "Final wapas lein" kare to band hui quotation wapas "Bhej di" ho jaati hain.
4) CUSTOMER KE LINK PAR HISTORY
   * "Aapko ab tak kya bheja gaya" (khulne wala dabba): pehle ki quotation, jo vikalp nahi chuna, apna badlav, final, aur rate revision.
5) STOCK RESERVE KE NIYAM
   * Ek option wali quotation: bhejte hi reserve (pehle jaisa).
   * Kai option wali quotation: Final (Accept) hone tak reserve NAHI. Final ke baad sirf chuna hua option reserve.
   * Band (superseded), expired ya rejected quotation ka reserve chhoot jata hai. Revision me sirf chalu rate ginta hai — double reserve nahi.
SEEMA (ISE SAAF JAAN LEIN):
   * Supabase me PDF files storage me padi rehti hain — app unhe delete nahi kar sakta (storage ki suraksha ke karan). Database ka bhaari data (links, PDF link) hat jata hai,
     par asli PDF files nahi. Storage bharne lage to Supabase dashboard -> Storage -> sp-images me purani PDF khud saaf kar sakte hain.
   * Pehle se padi purani quotations apne aap band nahi hongi, sirf aage ke naye Final par niyam lagega.

ROUND 43 — Inventory / Purchase price / Discount / Online qty (sirf STAGING)
==========================================================================
1) Kharid rate (Purchase price) ZAROORI
   - Naya product banate waqt "Kharid rate" ab main form me (More Info ke andar nahi). Khali chhodne par save nahi hoga.
   - Jis user ke paas "Kharid rate dekhna" permission nahi (staff), uske banaye product par "⚠ Kharid rate nahi" nishaan lagta hai;
     Admin/Manager bharega. Purane / Excel se aaye products par bhi yahi nishaan dikhega.
   - Bina kharid rate ke koi product Online Store ko bheja nahi ja sakta (server rokta hai).
2) Online ke liye qty CRM tay karta hai
   - "Store ko bhejein" dabane par poochta hai: online ke liye kitna maal alag (default = Settings wala hissa, 20%).
   - Utna maal "Online Store (auto)" naam se hold ho jata hai -> offline quotation ka formula sahi chalta hai. Dobara bhejne par naya qty purane ki jagah.
3) Preview / Review ho chuka to tick nahi
   - Jo product "In store review" ya "Live" ho chuka, uske saamne chunne wala tick ab nahi aata (dobara update ke liye uski apni "Store ko bhejein" button rahegi).
4) Discount limit role ke hisaab se
   - Manager 5%, Staff 2.5% (default). Settings > Bill / Quotation > "Discount aur Online Store hissa" me Admin badal sakta hai.
   - Kisi user ki alag limit Team me lagi ho to wahi chalegi.
   - Admin: koi fixed limit nahi, par rate kharid rate se neeche gaya to line ke neeche chetavni (kharid rate ke saath).
   - Manager / Staff: rate kharid rate se neeche jaa hi nahi sakta (server rokta hai).
5) Project Template ka maal alag reserve
   - Inventory/Stock dashboard me har item ke neeche "Template reserve: N · baaki (templates ke baad): M" dikhta hai.
   - "Kiske liye" list me Project Template ke naam bhi aate hain. Ye sirf gintI/dikhane ke liye hai — isse quotation rukti nahi.
6) Kharid rate ke bina Online bhejna / naya product Excel (starter catalog) se aaye to unhe baad me bharna hoga.

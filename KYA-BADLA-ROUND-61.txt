ROUND 61 — SARKARI SUBSIDY (SIRF JANKARI) + HAR PROJECT TYPE KE LIYE QUOTATION / BILL / ONLINE SHOP
===================================================================================================
PEHLE SAAF BAAT
- Subsidy Sunpowers nahi deta. Ye sirf Sarkari yojana ki JANKARI hai. Quotation / Bill / Order ki RAKAM kabhi KAM NAHI hoti.
- Subsidy sirf: RESIDENTIAL plant + ON-GRID ya HYBRID (Hybrid par "State ERC manzoori ke adheen" likha aata hai).
  Off-Grid, Commercial, Industrial, VFD / Pump, Service, Product — par subsidy kahin nahi dikhti.
- Online (Store App, Web Shop) aur Offline (CRM quotation / bill / customer link) — dono me ek hi data aur ek hi hisaab.

HISAAB (PM Surya Ghar, MNRE)
- Central: pehle 2 kW par ₹30,000/kW, agle 1 kW par ₹18,000, adhiktam 3 kW tak (max ₹78,000). Special category rajya ke alag dar Admin me badal sakte hain.
- Rajya subsidy: har rajya ka apna (fixed ya per-kW, upar cap). Customer ko Central + Rajya ka kul "anumanit" dikhta hai + "subsidy ke baad anumanit net kharch".
- Rajya subsidy jab tak aap Admin me "chalu" aur "verify" na karein, 0 maani jati hai (sirf Central dikhta hai).
  Abhi: UTTAR PRADESH chalu + verified (₹15,000/kW, max ₹30,000). DELHI (₹10,000/kW, max ₹30,000) aur BIHAR (₹10,000/kW, max ₹20,000) bhare hain par BAND — pehle sarkari site se verify karke chalu karein. Baaki rajya band.

ADMIN (Store Admin)
- Naya tab "🏛️ सब्सिडी": Central dar, calculator, aur 36 rajya ke card (dar, cap, verified, chalu — har ek ka alag save).
- Project / System ke product form me naya dabba: kW, System type (On-Grid / Hybrid / Off-Grid), "Residential" tick.
  Jis project me ye bhare honge wahi par customer ko subsidy dikhegi. Commercial / Industrial me Residential tick mat karein.
- CRM me Project template (Items) me "System type" select; Quotation ke upar bhi System type; Residential + kW + rajya milte hi neeche subsidy ka dabba apne aap aata hai aur bill / invoice me bhi chhapta hai ("rakam ghatai nahi gayi").

CUSTOMER
- Store App + Web Shop: pin code dalte hi product page / shop card / cart / checkout me "Sarkari subsidy (anumanit)" dikhti hai. Order ke baad customer ke order page par bhi jankari aur CRM order me bhi.
- Bina pin ke: "pin dalein, subsidy dikhegi" ka sanket.

FIX
- Delivery din double dikhta tha (Kahan bechna hai wala + Delivery Rate wala). Ab jahan "Kahan bechna hai" ka niyam hai wahi ek hi baar dikhta hai.

PEHLE YE KAREIN
- Admin → 🏛️ सब्सिडी me apne rajya ki dar official site (pmsuryaghar.gov.in / rajya DISCOM / nodal agency) se milakar verify tick karein, phir chalu.
- Project products (kW + System type + Residential) ek baar edit karke bharein.

ROUND 57 — CUSTOMER LINK PAR "PAY YOUR BILL AMOUNT" (QR + Account + Link, teeno ek saath)
==================================================================================
Kyun: customer ko shak na ho, aur staff apna alag account na bhej sake.
1) Bill link par: bill ki rakam, ab tak mili rakam, baaki (Rest) + UPI QR + company Bank account (Copy button) + payment link (agar Settings me daala).
2) Quotation link par: customer Accept kare to usi page par wahi dabba aata hai.
3) "Advance" shabd customer ko kahin nahi: sirf "Pay your bill amount". Pehle payment par koi limit nahi — QR me rakam locked nahi, customer jitni chahe de.
4) Dabba me likha hai: "Part-wise payment ki suvidha available hai" (customer ko lage ki hisson me bhi de sakte hain).
5) Sab details sirf Settings > Bill (Bank / UPI + Payment link) se. Badlav par Activity me "PAYMENT DETAILS badli" (kisne). Dabba band karne ka checkbox bhi wahi.
6) Bakaya Tax Invoice PDF par UPI QR bhi chhapta hai.
7) Reminder / Store Orders / online checkout me "Advance" ki jagah "bill amount / pehla payment".
NAHI BADLA: staff salary "Advance" (wo alag cheez hai), policy/terms pages ka legal likha text, step numbers, purana data.
Payment ki entry (Payment Received) ab bhi sirf aap CRM me confirm karke chadhate hain — link par koi paisa apne aap nahi chadhta.

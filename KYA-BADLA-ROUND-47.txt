   15  Loan / Finance   : bank, amount, status. Cash customer ke liye "Lagu nahi". "Disbursed" par
                          "Bill me payment jodein" -> payment form (loan ki rakam preset), payment_id save.
   16  Referral         : kisne refer kiya, kisko, reward. Reward dene par earning ban jati hai.
   17  AMC              : plan banao / renew; "AMC step poora hua" button. Renewal yaad rehta hai.
   18  Complaint        : ticket banao, status badlo, delete; reminder bhi.
   (Sab server se: crm_flow_rec, crm_fr_list/save/reward/del. Staff ki permission wahi jo flow step ki hai.)

2) COMMISSION -> SALARY (Team / Staffs -> Salary pay)
   - Pay modal me "Commission bhi jodein" tick: us staff ki due earnings salary me judti hain.
   - Payslip me Commission ki alag line + list me Commission column.
   - Salary pay hone par wo earnings "paid" ho jati hain; salary undo karne par wapas "due".
   - Plants / profile me "Salary me gayi" pill.

3) HAZRI (ATTENDANCE) + LEAVE
   - Profile me staff khud Check-in / Check-out kare; chhutti ki arji de (Leave apply).
   - Team -> "Hazri & Leave" tab: mahine ka grid, pending leaves Approve/Reject, cell edit.
   - Ravivaar weekly off. Manager = admin / staff.hr / staff.manage.
   - Salary kaatna sirf SUGGEST hota hai ("deduction suggest" button) — apne aap nahi kat'ta.

4) DOWNLOAD & SHARE DROPDOWN
   - Quotation / invoice view, ledger, reports, Documents list: alag alag buttons ki jagah ek dropdown
     (Download / Share / Excel / Accountant ko share). Esc ya bahar click se band; screen ke hisab se khulta hai.

5) STORE ADMIN -> CRM INVENTORY
   - Admin (store) me naya product banate waqt "CRM inventory me bhi jodein": category, cost, qty,
     Web / App tick, HSN. Item pehle CRM me bantaa hai, phir store product usse judta hai (dobara click par duplicate nahi).

6) EK HI PRICE HAR JAGAH
   - CRM item ka incl-GST price hi final. Store / Web ka MRP, offer, GST CRM se judi hone par lock (admin me 🔒 note).
   - CRM me price/GST badalte hi store ka price apne aap badalta hai.
   - Order ke waqt server check: cart ka rate purana ho to "PRICE_CHANGED" — Store app / Web checkout
     me saaf message + Refresh.

7) BLUEPRINT (Project blue print) — naya tareeka
   - "New blueprint" dabane par PEHLE customer chunna padta hai (list se, naya bhi bana sakte hain).
     Naam, mobile, pata, email, kW customer se apne aap bhar jaate hain; design usi customer ke flow me save hota hai.
   - Pehle step me ab: Plant type, Installation area type, Area LAMBAI & CHAUDAI, Area DIRECTION (N-S / E-W / tilted).
     Ye sab bhare bina aage nahi badh sakte (batata hai kya baaki hai).
   - Auto design me ye details dobara nahi puchhi jaati: area aur kW customer wale details se pehle se bhare aate hain.
   - Optional steps: Wiring (9), ACDB/DCDB/Earthing/Lightning arrestor (10), Cable tray (11) — "yeh nahi chahiye, skip karein" tick.
     Skip design ke saath save hota hai aur step "done" dikhta hai.
   - Save kiye bina kisi aur menu / "Back to list" par jaane par chetavni ("save nahi kiya, chhodna hai?");
     browser band/refresh par bhi browser chetavni deta hai.
   - Save hua design CRM me list me dikhta hai (kW, panels, sq ft), customer ke flow se khulta hai, delete ho sakta hai.
